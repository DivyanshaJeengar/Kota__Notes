Function Grapher Lab

How to use

  • Pick a curve: parabola, sine wave or exponential.
  • Drag the sliders for a, b, c. Each has its own colour in the equation.
  • Press play beside a slider to watch that number animate.
  • Drag the graph to move it. Scroll or use + and − to zoom.
  • Try Match the curve to test yourself.
  • Press Reset to start again.

Dashed grey: the starting curve. Move your pointer over the graph to read exact values.

Match the curve
