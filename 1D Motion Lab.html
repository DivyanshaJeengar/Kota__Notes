What to observe

  • With a = 0, the x–t graph is a straight line and its slope equals the velocity.
  • With a ≠ 0, the x–t graph curves. The slope at any moment is the velocity at that moment.
  • The shaded area under the v–t graph equals the displacement. Areas below zero count as negative.
  • Try v₀ = 10 and a = −2. The cart stops, turns around and comes back. Compare displacement with distance travelled.
