Acid–Base Titration Lab

Add titrant from the burette and watch the pH curve build.

How to use

  • Pick the solution, concentration and indicator.
  • Add titrant with the buttons, the slider, or Run.
  • Watch the flask colour and the pH curve change.
  • Find the steep jump: that is the equivalence point.
  • Press Reset to start again.

Set up the experiment

Titrant: 0.100 M NaOH

Try these

  1. Titrate HCl, then acetic acid at the same concentration. Compare the starting pH and the pH at equivalence.
  2. On the weak acid, move to exactly half the equivalence volume. The pH there equals the pKa (4.76).
  3. Change the indicator. Its shaded colour band on the graph should sit inside the steep jump. Which indicators fail for the weak acid?
  4. Double the concentration. What happens to the equivalence volume, but not to the curve's shape?
